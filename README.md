# Uber Rides Data Analysis (Power BI)

## 📊 Overview
This project analyzes Uber trip data using Power BI dashboards.  
It provides insights into revenue generation, payment distribution, and trip trends by time and location.

## 🔑 Key Metrics
- Total Trips: 116  
- Total Fare: $1.8M  
- Total Duration: 1.9M  
- Total Distance: 393  
- Night Shift Percentage: 8.7%  

## 📈 Visualizations
- **Fare by Location:** Highest fares from Penn Station/Madison ($71,197.7) and LaGuardia Airport ($54,342.3).  
- **Payment Type:** Majority of payments via Uber Pay (70.75%), followed by Cash (28.51%).  
- **Shift Analysis:** Day shifts dominate (89.93%) compared to night shifts (10.07%).  
- **Daily Trip Trend:** Peak trips on Sunday and Saturday (~14K each).  
- **Hourly Trend:** Trips consistent throughout the day, with demand rising after 9 AM.  

## 🎯 Insights
- Day shifts generate the majority of revenue.  
- Uber Pay is the most preferred payment method.  
- Penn Station and LaGuardia Airport are top revenue‑generating locations.  
- Weekend trips (Saturday & Sunday) show the highest demand.
